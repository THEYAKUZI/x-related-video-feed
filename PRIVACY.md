# Privacy Policy: Related Video Feed for X

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

Related Video Feed for X ("the extension") does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data.

## What the extension does

- It only runs on x.com.
- It asks x.com for related videos, and for like/bookmark actions you choose, using the X session
  you're already logged in with, the same way the x.com website itself does. These requests go
  directly from your browser to X. They never pass through any server run by the extension's developer.
- It loads videos and images directly from X's own servers (`video.twimg.com`, `pbs.twimg.com`).

## What is stored

- Two player settings (muted on/off, auto-next on/off) are saved in your browser with Chrome's
  extension storage, so they're remembered next time. They never leave your device.

## What is not collected

- No analytics, tracking, ads or third-party services.
- No personal information, browsing history, X account details or passwords are sent to the
  developer or anyone else.
